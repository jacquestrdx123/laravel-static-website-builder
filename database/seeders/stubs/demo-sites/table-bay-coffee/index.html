The roastery

A 12 kg drum and a lot of note-taking

We started in 2017 with a second-hand roaster and a stall at the Neighbourgoods Market. The stall is long gone; the roaster is still here, on Albert Road, running four days a week.

We buy through importers who publish what they paid the farm. It isn't a marketing line — it's on the bag, and if you ask us we'll show you the contract.
