About

I'd rather you forget I'm there

I'm Naledi. I've been photographing weddings around Gauteng and the Free State since 2016, after five years of news and editorial work — which is where the documentary habit comes from.

I work in natural light almost entirely, and I stay out of the way. The couples I work best with are the ones who want to spend the day with their people rather than on a two-hour photo shoot away from their own celebration.

You don't need 2 000 photographs. You need the eighty that you'll still want to look at in twenty years.

Every booking includes a curated, edited gallery — not a memory card of everything the shutter caught. Culling is part of the work.
