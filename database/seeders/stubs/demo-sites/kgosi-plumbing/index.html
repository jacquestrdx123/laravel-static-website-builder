Questions we get asked

What does the callout fee actually cover?

Travel plus the first 30 minutes on site — normally enough to stop a leak and diagnose the problem. Any repair beyond that is quoted before we continue.

Do you issue a Certificate of Compliance?

Yes, on all geyser installations and replacements. We're PIRB registered, so the CoC is valid for insurance and for the sale of a property.

My insurer wants a report. Can you provide one?

We do this regularly. You'll get a written assessment with photographs, suitable for a claim, usually within one working day of the callout.

Which areas do you cover?

Pretoria East mainly: Faerie Glen, Garsfontein, Menlo Park, Lynnwood, Silver Lakes and surrounds. Further afield we'll be honest about whether we can get there in time.

How soon can you come out?

Emergencies, typically within 90 minutes. Non-urgent work is usually booked within two to three working days.
